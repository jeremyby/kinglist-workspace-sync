King List Drive Sync

A private, one-way mirror of the owner's King List project files to an owner-designated Google Drive folder.

Purpose

This utility is operated by Jeremy for his own project. It uses rclone on a private computer to copy selected project files to Google Drive and reconcile later changes. It is not a public file-sharing service or the King List application.

Account access

The owner authorizes access directly with Google. The utility can inspect destination files and create, update or trash files in its configured mirror folder. Its requested Drive permission can cover the account's Drive files; the configured destination is an operational restriction, not a folder-only OAuth permission.

The mirror is one-way. Changes made directly in the destination may be overwritten or removed during synchronization. It is not a versioned backup.

Private files stay private

This public website provides information only. It does not receive Google authorization tokens or publish the mirrored files. No account sign-in, uploads or synchronization can be performed through this website.

Policies and contact

Read the privacy policy and terms of use. The operator's public contact profile is Jeremy on GitHub. Do not post credentials or private project files in public issues.
