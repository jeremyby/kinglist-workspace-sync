Terms of use

Personal use only

King List Drive Sync is operated by Jeremy for his own authorized files and Google account. It is not offered as a public synchronization service. This website provides information only and does not grant access to the private King List project.

One-way synchronization

The local workspace is the synchronization source. Destination files can be created, overwritten or moved to trash to match that source. Destination-only edits are not imported into the local workspace. A mirror is not a versioned backup, an archival guarantee or an atomic snapshot of actively changing files.

Owner responsibilities

The owner is responsible for choosing the source and destination, authorizing the Google account, reviewing exclusions, protecting credentials, ensuring rights to store the selected material, maintaining adequate Drive storage and keeping any necessary independent backups.

Availability and third-party services

The utility is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted operation or error-free synchronization. Network failures, authorization expiry, service quotas and file changes can delay or interrupt transfers. Google Drive, GitHub Pages and rclone remain subject to their respective applicable terms and licenses.

Stopping use

The owner may stop synchronization, revoke Google access and remove mirrored data at any time. See the privacy policy for data handling and removal details.
